Grandma's Pancakes

Breakfast ⏱️ 20 mins

Fluffy, golden pancakes that have been a family favorite for three generations. Perfect for lazy Sunday mornings.

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 2 eggs
  • 1¾ cups milk
  • ¼ cup melted butter

Instructions:

  1. Mix dry ingredients in a large bowl
  2. Whisk eggs, milk, and melted butter in another bowl
  3. Combine wet and dry ingredients until just mixed
  4. Heat griddle to medium-high and lightly grease
  5. Pour ¼ cup batter for each pancake
  6. Cook until bubbles form, flip and cook until golden

Mom's Spaghetti Bolognese

Main Course ⏱️ 45 mins

A rich and hearty meat sauce that simmers to perfection. This is comfort food at its finest.

Ingredients:

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 28 oz crushed tomatoes
  • 6 oz tomato paste
  • 1 cup red wine
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoon dried basil
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 lb spaghetti

Instructions:

  1. Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium heat
  2. Sauté onion until soft, add garlic and cook 1 minute
  3. Add ground beef and cook until browned
  4. Stir in tomato paste and cook 2 minutes
  5. Add crushed tomatoes, wine, and seasonings
  6. Simmer for 30 minutes, stirring occasionally
  7. Cook spaghetti according to package directions
  8. Serve sauce over spaghetti with parmesan cheese

Aunt Sally's Chocolate Chip Cookies

Dessert ⏱️ 25 mins

Crispy on the outside, chewy on the inside - these cookies are legendary at family gatherings.

Ingredients:

  • 2¼ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup butter, softened
  • ¾ cup granulated sugar
  • ¾ cup packed brown sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 2 cups chocolate chips

Instructions:

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C)
  2. Combine flour, baking soda, and salt
  3. Beat butter and both sugars until creamy
  4. Add eggs and vanilla, beat well
  5. Gradually blend in flour mixture
  6. Stir in chocolate chips
  7. Drop rounded tablespoons onto ungreased cookie sheets
  8. Bake 9-11 minutes until golden brown

Grandpa's Sourdough Bread

Baking ⏱️ 24 hours

A tangy, crusty sourdough with a soft interior. This recipe has been perfected over decades.

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup active sourdough starter
  • 1½ cups warm water
  • 4 cups bread flour
  • 2 teaspoons salt

Instructions:

  1. Mix starter and water in a large bowl
  2. Add flour and mix until shaggy dough forms
  3. Let rest 30 minutes (autolyse)
  4. Add salt and knead until smooth
  5. Let rise 8-12 hours at room temperature
  6. Shape into a round loaf
  7. Place in banneton, proof 2-4 hours
  8. Bake at 450°F for 30-40 minutes

Nana's Apple Pie

Dessert ⏱️ 1 hour

The quintessential American dessert with a flaky crust and cinnamon-spiced apple filling.

Ingredients:

  • 2 pie crusts (homemade or store-bought)
  • 6-7 cups sliced apples (Granny Smith)
  • ¾ cup sugar
  • 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • ¼ teaspoon nutmeg
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1 egg (for egg wash)

Instructions:

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C)
  2. Line pie dish with one crust
  3. Mix apples, sugar, flour, cinnamon, and nutmeg
  4. Fill pie crust with apple mixture
  5. Dot with butter
  6. Cover with second crust, seal and crimp edges
  7. Cut slits in top, brush with egg wash
  8. Bake 40-50 minutes until golden and bubbly

Uncle Joe's BBQ Chicken

Main Course ⏱️ 50 mins

Tender, juicy chicken with a sweet and tangy BBQ sauce. Perfect for summer cookouts.

Ingredients:

  • 4 lbs chicken pieces
  • 2 cups BBQ sauce
  • ¼ cup brown sugar
  • 2 tablespoons apple cider vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon onion powder
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions:

  1. Season chicken with salt, pepper, garlic and onion powder
  2. Mix BBQ sauce, brown sugar, vinegar, and Worcestershire
  3. Preheat grill to medium heat
  4. Grill chicken 6-8 minutes per side
  5. Brush with sauce during last 10 minutes
  6. Cook until internal temp reaches 165°F
  7. Let rest 5 minutes before serving
